WHAT IS NEW
1. Athleticism, college production, and overall prospect scores are selectable in
Research Lab comparisons, correlation rankings, percentile charts and similar prospects.
2. Model Lab has NGS baseline presets: athleticism + college production, or overall
prospect score alone. Test additional variables against these baselines.
3. College pathways compares schools or conferences within a position and draft classes.
Choose an NFL outcome, minimum outcome observations and minimum players per group.
4. The adjusted tab shows player-held-out predictions from your selected baseline
and each school's average outcome above that baseline. School is not used as a predictor.
5. Plain-language explanations distinguish Combine IQ (a dashboard) from NGS scores,
app sample percentiles, official NFL forecasts, and model-derived expectations.

INTERPRETATION
All NFL comparisons here use rookie regular-season outcomes. Source data includes selected
prospects, not entire college output. No causal school-development claims. Player-held-out
checks are exploratory after variable selection, not independent validation. Score snapshot
and model training history are unverified. Overall prospect score overlaps with its components.
Missing scores/outcomes are excluded and counted. Outcome averages give each player equal weight.
School/conference labels are used as supplied, without assigning value across transfers.
